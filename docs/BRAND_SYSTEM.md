# KALLISTI Rebrand System

## Core idea

**To the Most Beautiful.**

The iOS icon is a pen-and-ink illustration of a Greek goddess marble relief — Kristie rendered as Aphrodite, profile facing right, hair down and flowing, sunburst halo, carved stone medallion frame. The complete identity carries that classical feminine power into a native iOS product:

- monumental, not decorative
- artistic illustration, not photorealistic
- private and self-hosted, not anonymous "AI cloud" imagery
- luxurious, platinum-on-obsidian aesthetic

Primary line: **To the Most Beautiful.**

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Obsidian | `#0C0C10` | default app background |
| Deep Ink | `#000000` | deepest background, OLED |
| Slate | `#16181C` | panels and large dark fields |
| Charcoal | `#1C1D22` | cards and controls |
| Raised | `#22232A` | selected/raised surfaces |
| Selected | `#2A2B33` | selected state |
| Platinum | `#C8CCD2` | primary interactive / accent |
| Platinum Hot | `#E5E8EC` | focus, active status |
| Bone | `#F0F2F5` | primary foreground |
| Mist | `#A8ADB5` | secondary text |
| Pewter | `#6B7078` | tertiary text |
| Divider | `#2A2B33` | low-opacity rules and borders |

Gold and blue are not part of the Kallisti identity. Keep semantic success, warning, and danger colors functional and rare.

## Typography

- Display and marketing: New York / Georgia, restrained and editorial.
- Native UI: SF Pro or system default for dense reading.
- Labels, status, code, metadata: SF Mono or Menlo, uppercase with modest tracking.
- Never set long paragraphs in all caps or monospaced text.

## Texture

- Use the goddess illustration as watermark in large atmospheric fields, chat wallpaper, onboarding, and marketing.
- Keep text-bearing app surfaces clean.
- Texture opacity in-product: 3-10%.
- Texture opacity in marketing: 15-45%.
- No fake marble rendering, literal stone walls, or mythological prop clutter.

## Shape language

- Cards: 12-18 pt corners.
- Composer and compact controls: 22-28 pt.
- Pills only for chips, statuses, and primary compact actions.
- Borders are hairlines with low contrast; avoid bright outlines.

## Logo use

- The seal (goddess medallion) is the compact brand mark.
- The wordmark is the primary horizontal identity.
- Do not place the seal over a busy crop without a dark scrim.
- Keep clear space around the seal equal to 12% of its diameter.
- Minimum digital seal size: 32 px; use the full app icon above 64 px.

## Kallisti v2.5.1 theme system

Version **2.5.1** introduces three formal tracks:

### 1. Kallisti Dark (Default)
The flagship brand theme:
- obsidian foundation (#0C0C10)
- platinum accent (#C8CCD2)
- bone foreground (#F0F2F5)
- mist and pewter for secondary hierarchy
- subtle goddess watermark at 10% opacity in chat wallpaper
- ideal for daily use, the canonical product look

### 2. Kallisti OLED
A companion dark theme for maximum contrast and battery efficiency:
- true black backgrounds (#000000 / #050507)
- more restrained watermark (6% opacity)
- sharper edge separation between cards and chrome
- brighter platinum focus states
- intended for chat-heavy and late-night use

### 3. Kallisti Light
A clean light mode:
- near-white backgrounds (#FAFAFA)
- obsidian foreground
- same platinum-family accent logic inverted
- elegant, editorial, not harsh

All three should feel like **the same product** — a display variant family, not separate identities.
